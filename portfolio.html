Workflow

Our Design Process

01

Spatial Discovery

We align on your habits, preferences, and light configurations. We inspect the layout and run site shadow evaluations.

02

3D Spatial Mapping

We construct detailed structural configurations. We map sightlines, circulation paths, and artificial light distributions.

03

Material Curation

We source raw, organic materials—reclaimed oaks, neutral lime wall plasters, textured linen fibers, and custom metals.

04

Artisanal Styling

We install custom fixtures, mount handcrafted stone slabs, adjust the warm dimming details, and style details for final reveals.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the typical timeline for an interior transformation?

Residential transformations usually require 8 to 12 weeks from initial site survey to final delivery, depending on custom joinery and mason timelines. Workspace layouts typically take 10 to 14 weeks.

Do you source custom, one-of-a-kind furniture pieces?

Yes, we collaborate directly with independent woodworkers, stone sculptors, and ceramists. We design bespoke fixtures, dining tables, and shelving and supervise their handcrafting.

How do you ensure sustainable design choices?

We select low-VOC plasters, FSC-certified woods, recyclable metal hardware, and hand-woven fibers. We aim to design timeless interiors that outlive short-term fast-fashion trends, reducing physical waste.
