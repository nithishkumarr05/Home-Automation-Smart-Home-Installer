our story

Where Spatial Harmony
Meets Human Soul

Founded in 2018, Aura Space was born from a vision to liberate interiors from sterile configurations. We believe a room is not just a layout of furniture, but a living sanctuary that shapes your daily rhythm, thoughts, and peace.

our philosophy

The Three Pillars of Aura

Organic Minimalism

Reducing noise to let nature speak. We prioritize natural woods, stones, and breathable textiles to keep spaces physically and mentally light.

Spatial Wisdom

We structure configurations to follow human movement. Every path, sightline, and entry point of light is carefully orchestrated for balance.

Artisanal Honesty

Rejecting mass-produced sterility. We work closely with local artisans to craft distinct hardware, furniture, and custom masonry.

How We Evolved

100+ Completed Havens

2026

Rebranding to Aura Space

Repositioned as Aura Space, centering on dynamic, light-adaptive premium architecture and organic, sensory-focused home concepts.
